Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how Memory Game: Place Objects ("the game") handles information. In short: the game does not collect your personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: for example your best results, unlocked worlds and levels, and settings such as language and sound. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the game.

Advertising and third parties

The game does not show ads, does not use advertising or tracking services and does not share any information with third parties.

Internet access

The game works fully offline and does not send data over the internet.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
