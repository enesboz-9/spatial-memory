Memory Game: Place Objects

Study a scene, watch the objects disappear, then drag each one back to where it stood. The closer you place it, the higher you score.

Common questions

How is my score calculated?
Each object is scored by how close you place it to its original position. The result screen shows the actual position next to yours, with the accuracy for every object.
How do I unlock new worlds and levels?
Keep playing and improve your results. New worlds and difficulty levels unlock as you progress.
Is my progress saved?
Yes, your progress and records are saved on your device. If you delete the app, they are removed with it.
Does the game need an internet connection or an account?
No. The game works offline and does not ask you to sign in.
